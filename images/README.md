- `images/projects/ledgerly.jpg` — Ledgerly (index.html + projects.html)
- `images/projects/pipecraft.jpg` — Pipecraft (index.html + projects.html)
- `images/projects/northlane.jpg` — Northlane (index.html + projects.html)
- `images/projects/fitscore.jpg` — Fitscore (projects.html)
- `images/projects/streamline.jpg` — Streamline (projects.html)
- `images/projects/craftmarket.jpg` — Craftmarket (projects.html)
- `images/articles/idempotent-payment-apis.jpg` — article thumbnail (index.html)
- `images/articles/cicd-orchestrator.jpg` — article thumbnail (index.html)
- `images/articles/multi-tenant-data-models.jpg` — article thumbnail (index.html)
