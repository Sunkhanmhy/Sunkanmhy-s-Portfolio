A few principles I don't compromise on

Tests before demos

A feature isn't done until it's covered — I'd rather ship a week later than hand off something fragile.

Clear over clever

The smartest one-liner loses to the version the next engineer can read at 2am during an incident.

Measure, don't guess

Every performance claim gets a number attached. If I can't measure it, I don't ship it as a fix.
